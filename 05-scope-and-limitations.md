# Honest scope and limitations

- Scope: plant–pollinator (flower visitation), CONUS, post-2013. The phenophase is **flowering** throughout; other interaction types (frugivory, herbivory) are out of scope.
- The claim is interaction *opportunity* (temporal/spatial co-occurrence), never interaction strength or fitness (Blanchet; Coelho's ~20%). State this in the abstract.
- Held-out positives and PPE share a peak-bias, so the eval shows "PPE predicts *where observed interactions* occur, including at unobserved sites," **not** "recovers unbiased full opportunity including shoulders." Scope to the former.
- The pollinator side is the weak half (no PPE-equivalent; biased, sparse occurrence data). It is a needed term, defended by the sensitivity ablation (#5 in [experiments](03-experiments-and-ablations.md)), not a contribution. Pre-check: how many CONUS bee/butterfly species have enough GBIF dates for a stable activity window? If too few even for this, fall back to a plant-only phenology analysis.
- Novelty is the *input*, not the framework. Position against Poisot/Dormann explicitly; do not claim the framework or the across-space prediction as new.
